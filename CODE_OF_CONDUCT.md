# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone. The full text of the pledge, our standards, and how enforcement works is in the Contributor Covenant linked above.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders at rory.mc@verifiablesystems.org.
